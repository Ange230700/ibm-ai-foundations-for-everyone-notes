# S07 — End-to-End Professional Workshop — 60-Minute Content Contract

## Status and explicit draft decisions

- **Position:** APPLY, integrating S01–S06.
- **Audience:** professionals without technical prerequisites.
- **Draft format:** 60 minutes, 25 aligned slides in EN and FR.
- **Participation:** individual or paired work, with equivalent offline participation.
- **Assessment:** formative feedback, not a certification or scored examination.
- **Working aid:** localized reference extracts with a twelve-field record, no separate workbook.
- **Readiness:** implementation draft; instructor review, native playback and rehearsal remain pending.
- **Parent:** `docs/teaching/ai-for-everyone-professional-curriculum.md`.

The parent curriculum left duration, participation, assessment and handout decisions open.
The choices above are explicit implementation proposals for this draft, not decisions attributed
to the user or silently applied across the whole program. Final program title remains open.

## Role and outcomes

S07 applies the previous sessions in one bounded task. It introduces no new model taxonomy,
security standard, law or vendor capability. Learners choose a reception handover or follow-up
checklist from the same fictional packet. A pre-approved wholly fictional alternative may be used
when it has equivalent evidence, permissions and scope.

Learners must demonstrate a defined result and audience, permitted input selection, a prompt,
a retained first output, a justified iteration, independent source checking, named human
responsibility, a reusable method, an honest value statement and explicit limits.

## Exact exercise evidence and permissions

The localized `reference-extracts.md` files are the complete learner packet. Keep A–D and R1–R5
aligned in meaning and identity across languages. Source A has exactly two entries dated
19 October 2026: SB-701 at 480 kg and SB-702 with no recorded weight, marked needs completion.
Source B supplies the missing-field review rule. Source C records a request to the reception lead,
with no reply, corrected weight or completion time. Source D is wholly fictional and excluded.

The packet permits A–C in a simulated drafting step only. A real service additionally requires
actual authorization. A staff member verifies; the operations lead approves internal circulation;
the information owner resolves permission questions. Approval remains pending throughout the
exercise. Lot acceptance, grading, payments and external sending remain outside scope.

Neither association with a lot nor a missing field establishes responsibility or blame. Preserve
unknown values and avoid importing facts or permissions from S04–S06.

## Workshop modes and retained evidence

In live mode retain the actual prompt and response. In offline mode write the prompt, use the
supplied flawed response and label it as supplied simulation. During iteration, manually compose
and label a revised simulation. Never claim that a supplied response resulted from the learner's
prompt. A good live response does not need an invented failure: improve usability or use the
challenge card as an additional test.

The packet must be available independently of the projector throughout practice. In pairs, switch
writer and checker during iteration. Solo learners perform a separate source-based review pass.
The final deliverable is a process record, not just a final answer. Keep all twelve fields from the
parent capstone contract, including both versions and reasons for change.

## Value and limits

The numerical illustration is explicitly fictional: manual drafting plus checking is 12 minutes;
assisted preparation and drafting is 4, checking and correction is 6, total 10. Difference: 2 minutes,
about 16.7 percent of the manual baseline. The comparison assumes equivalent scope and quality.
No real productivity gain is established. Approval waiting is tracked separately where relevant.
Without an observed comparable baseline, the learner reports an unestablished benefit and designs
the next comparison. Offline simulation does not measure model performance.

Missing data need not block a transparent draft. They block claims that rely on treating the data
as known. Permission ambiguity stops sharing; missing approval stops circulation. State each limit
against the particular action and responsible role.
