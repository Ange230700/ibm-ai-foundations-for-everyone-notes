## Session objective

Recognize bounded professional tasks where generative AI can assist, choose an output suited to the
need and audience, preserve supplied facts during transformation, and identify what a responsible
person must verify, approve, or use.

## Facilitation principles

1. Start from recognizable professional work before discussing output types or tools.
2. Present generated content as a draft to review rather than an automatically approved result.
3. Keep the fictional cocoa cooperative near Soubré as the anchor case while transferring the
   method to other professional contexts.
4. Preserve supplied facts when rewriting, summarizing, reorganizing, or changing format.
5. Treat missing information as missing rather than permission to invent it.
6. Use only fictional, anonymized, public, or explicitly authorized information in activities.
7. Keep detailed prompt engineering for S03 and deeper document analysis for S04.
8. Make human verification, approval, authorization, or use explicit whenever the task requires it.

### Teaching Notes

**Key takeaway:** AI can transform the form of content without receiving permission to invent missing facts.
