## Workshop record — twelve fields

1. **Problem:** Which bounded work problem are you addressing?
2. **Expected result:** Handover or checklist? What makes it useful?
3. **Audience:** Who will read it?
4. **Allowed information:** Which sources enter the drafting step? What is excluded and why?
5. **Prompt or sequence:** Record your exact instructions and permitted input.
6. **Example output:** Keep version 1. Label it actual, supplied simulation or manually simulated.
7. **Iteration:** What changed in your instructions or workflow? Why? Keep version 2 and its label.
8. **Verification:** Complete the claim check below, including important claims in version 2.
9. **Human responsibility:** Name the drafter, reviewer, approver and escalation role. State approval status.
10. **Reuse:** What stays in the template? What must be replaced and rechecked next time?
11. **Value:** Record time and quality evidence. Label measured, estimated or simulated figures.
    If there is no comparable baseline, state that benefit is unestablished and plan a comparison.
12. **Limitations:** Which action must stop under which condition, and who resolves it?
