# S06 quick reference: responsible AI-assisted work

## Three checks before use or sharing

- **Evidence:** Is each important statement supported by an allowed source? Preserve what is missing or unknown.
- **Permission:** May these inputs be used in this tool for this purpose and with these recipients?
- **Authority:** Who may review, approve circulation, or make the operational decision?

A “yes” to one check does not satisfy the other two. If permission is unclear, stop and ask the information owner before sharing.

## Safe review sequence

1. Define the purpose, minimum necessary information, tool, and recipients.
2. Exclude restricted and unnecessary information before drafting.
3. Compare important claims with the permitted sources; record corrections and unresolved questions.
4. Let the designated reviewer return or stop unsupported work.
5. Obtain approval for the specific circulation. Do not treat approval of a brief as approval of a lot, storage, quality, payment, or personnel decision.
6. Get a separate explicit decision before external sharing or adding recipients.
7. If restricted information was already shared, stop further circulation and notify the information owner. Do not assume deletion reverses exposure.

## Preserve source limits

For the fictional S06 packet:

- Source A gives a pause at 09:10 and a resumption at 09:40; the reason is not stated.
- Source B records 540 kg for SB-601. SB-602 has a missing weight and requires staff review; the cause is not identified.
- Source C records SB-601's movement at 12:15 to Area A, a destination label only.
- The packet does not establish acceptance, quality, storage approval, payment, or responsibility for the missing field.
- Exclude restricted Source D in full under the exercise rule. Do not copy its contents into a tool, brief, or review record.
