## Slide 07 — Read the rules before sharing

- **Identifier:** S06-07
- **Duration:** 3 minutes

### On-Slide Content

Fictional rules for this exercise

- R1: Tool A receives operational facts A–C only
- R2: exclude all of restricted attachment D
- R3: preserve unknowns and avoid inferred decisions
- R7: ask the information owner when permission is unclear

### Teaching Notes

**Key takeaway:** Tool A is a fictional permission label. No vendor behavior is specified. Credentials are represented only by a placeholder in D, and no real secret may be used. The lesson operates with printed or local reference material, without real uploading.

**Learner action / expected response:** Read R1–R3 and R7 with the class. Explain that the complete R1–R8 card stays accessible during practice.

**Misconception:** The policy card grants only the permissions it actually states.

**Timing:** 3 minutes, including explanation and learner action.

**Source basis:** Canonical Module 04; S06 content contract, slide S06-07.

**R1** Tool A is permitted only for drafting this internal brief from the operational facts in A–C. All live tool use is simulated in class.

**R2** Source D and its personal, location, payment, and secret fields must not enter Tool A or the brief. Replacing a name does not authorize the remaining fields.

**R3** Preserve missing information. Do not infer causes, responsibility, approval, quality, or payment decisions.

**R4** The staff reviewer checks the draft against A–C and records corrections. The reviewer may return or stop the draft.

**R5** The operations lead approves circulation to the internal operations team. Approval of the brief does not approve lots, storage, payments, certificates, or personnel decisions.

**R6** External sharing and new recipients require an explicit decision from the operations lead before sending. Existing internal approval does not extend to them.

**R7** Unclear input permission goes to the information owner. If restricted information has already been shared, stop further sharing and notify the information owner, who coordinates the response. Do not assume deletion reverses exposure.

**R8** Record the issue, correction, reviewer, approval state, and unresolved questions in the restricted internal review record. Do not copy Source D into that record; refer to its source label only.
